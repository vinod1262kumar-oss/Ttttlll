ABOUT US

We're Building the Future of Food Clarity

TruthLens exists because food marketing has become disconnected from reality. We're fixing that.

Our Mission

Most people can't read a nutrition label—or don't trust what they see. Marketing hides behind "natural," "healthy," and "wholesome" while nutrition facts stay buried in tiny print. We're here to close that gap.

TruthLens uses AI to instantly extract, decode, and verify every claim on a package. We show you what's really inside—sugar, salt, fat—in clear language. No jargon. No bias. Just facts.

Why This Matters

💡 Knowledge is Power

You make 200+ food choices a week. Most happen without full information. We give you the data to make choices that align with your actual values.

🎯 Transparency Wins

When consumers can see the truth, brands have to compete on quality, not just marketing. That's better for everyone.

🔐 Privacy First

Your food data is personal. We don't sell it. We don't track you. Your scans stay in your private account.

🚀 Tech for Good

We use the latest in AI (Gemini vision), cloud infrastructure (Supabase), and security (Helmet, RLS) responsibly.

What We're Building

V1 is the foundation: instant label scanning, nutrition analysis, and claim verification. But we're just getting started.

Future versions will include meal planning, dietary pattern tracking, and community insights—all built on the same privacy-first, transparency-driven philosophy.
