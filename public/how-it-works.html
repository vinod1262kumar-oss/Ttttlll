HOW IT WORKS

From Package to Clarity in 4 Steps

1

📸 Scan the Label

Take a clear photo of any packaged food's nutrition label. Our AI recognizes:

  • • Serving size & number of servings
  • • Calories and all nutrients
  • • Ingredients list
  • • Marketing claims & certifications
2

🔍 AI Analyzes

Gemini AI reads the image and extracts structured nutrition data. We normalize values and flag:

  • • Sugar vs. daily guidelines
  • • Sodium levels
  • • Trans fat & saturated fat
  • • Unusual ingredient patterns
3

⚖️ Claims vs. Reality

We compare what the package says to what the nutrition facts show:

  • • "No Added Sugar" but high carbs?
  • • "Natural" with 15 ingredients?
  • • "Wholesome" but high sodium?
  • • We flag inconsistencies
4

✅ You Decide

Get a clear verdict on whether this product fits your goals:

  • • Health status: Lower / Watch / High
  • • What nutrient is the main concern
  • • How many grams you can safely have
  • • Saved to your private history

What Happens Behind the Scenes?

When you submit an image, your phone sends it securely to our backend. We never store raw images—only the extracted nutrition data and your analysis. Gemini AI processes the image server-side, and the results are encrypted in Supabase. Your data is protected by Row Level Security (RLS), meaning only you can access your scans. We use Helmet for CSP, rate limiting to prevent abuse, and atomic database functions to lock your quota.
